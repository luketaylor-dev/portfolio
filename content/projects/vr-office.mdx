# VR Office

A **proof-of-concept virtual office** built in Unity to explore the future of collaborative remote work in VR.  
The project combined the **Meta SDK** with Unity’s **XR Interaction Toolkit** to deliver natural input handling, spatial UI, and interactive environments.

## Overview

The VR Office was designed as a technical exploration of how immersive environments could move beyond gaming and entertainment.  
The focus was on creating a shared, physics-rich workspace where multiple users could collaborate naturally inside VR.

## Key Features

- **Collaborative Workspaces** – multi-user synchronisation with Netcode for GameObjects
- **Physics-Based Interactions** – tactile manipulation of props (whiteboards, chairs, tools)
- **Persistent Environments** – early tests of long-running sessions and cross-device continuity

## Technical Implementation

- Built in **Unity** with **C#**
- Integrated **Meta SDK** for headset and controller support
- Used **XR Interaction Toolkit** for intuitive VR interactions
- Multi-user networking powered by **Netcode for GameObjects**
- Physics-based systems for realistic object handling and immersion

## Why It Matters

This project explored how VR can extend into **collaborative, professional settings**. By experimenting with shared physics-based environments, it highlighted how immersive tech could support productivity, creativity, and remote teamwork.

## Future Potential

- Integration with real-world productivity tools (e.g. shared documents, cloud storage)
- Expanded social presence with avatars and gesture tracking
- Scalability for larger teams and enterprise use cases

---

The VR Office served as a **technical prototype**, demonstrating how immersive VR environments can provide new ways of working and collaborating remotely.
