# VR Office

A **proof-of-concept virtual office** created in Unity to explore collaborative remote work in VR. The project combined the **Meta SDK** with Unity’s **XR Interaction Toolkit** to deliver natural input handling and spatial UI.

## Overview

The VR Office was built as a technical experiment in how VR environments could extend beyond entertainment. It focused on creating a shared, interactive workspace where users could collaborate in real time.

## Highlights

- **Collaborative Workspaces** – multi-user synchronisation with Multiplayer
- **Physics-Based Props** – interactive whiteboards, chairs, and tools
- **Persistent Environments** – testing continuity across longer VR sessions

## Technical Implementation

- Unity + C# with Meta SDK integration
- XR Interaction Toolkit for intuitive object interaction
- Networking via Multiplayer
- Physics-driven systems for immersion

## Why It Matters

This prototype explored the potential of VR for professional collaboration, testing how immersive environments can support **productivity, creativity, and teamwork** beyond gaming.

## Future Potential

- Integration with real-world productivity tools (docs, cloud storage)
- Expanded avatars and social presence systems
- Scaling to larger teams and enterprise collaboration
