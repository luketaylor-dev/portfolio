# Baa Baa Bomber

As **Co-Founder and Lead Developer**, I prototyped **Baa Baa Bomber** – a comedic indie title where players take command of Captain _Minty Woolcock_ of the Royal Sheep Air Force. The mission: defend the homeland from invading evil pigeons with precision bombing runs, slapstick physics, and absurd humour.

## Overview

The project combined light-hearted game design with serious technical experimentation. At its core, it explored how physics-based gameplay and unconventional input methods (like EEG controls) could be fused into a mobile-friendly, free-to-play experience.

## Gameplay Modes

- **Core Physics Campaign**  
  Puzzle-based bombing missions inspired by _Angry Birds_, featuring destructible pigeon fortresses that escalate in complexity.

- **Infinite Scroller Variant**  
  A fast-paced arcade mode where players dodge obstacles and bomb pigeons endlessly to chase high scores.

- **EEG Integration (Experimental)**  
  Prototype builds connected gameplay to brainwave signals via BrainFlow, enabling “hands-free” bombing actions as a test of experimental control systems.

## Technical Implementation

- Built in **Unity** with **C#**
- **Physics-driven bombing system** with realistic trajectories and destructible structures
- Dual gameplay frameworks (puzzle campaign + endless arcade)
- **EEG input experiments** linking real-world brainwave activity to in-game triggers
- Mobile-first design targeting **iOS/Android** with free-to-play mechanics in mind

## Impact

- Served as a **creative experiment** in comedy-driven design, blending absurd narrative with accessible gameplay
- Functioned as a **technical testbed** for EEG input in casual mobile games
- Provided a strong prototype foundation for exploring F2P mechanics and player retention strategies

## Future Potential

- Expansion into a full campaign with narrative progression and unlockable content
- Refinement of EEG controls for accessibility and novelty gameplay modes
- Possible integration of social/competitive features (leaderboards, seasonal events)

---

**Baa Baa Bomber** remains in prototype stage, but demonstrates how humour, physics systems, and emerging control technologies can be combined to explore new directions in casual game design.
