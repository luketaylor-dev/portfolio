# Free-to-Play Poker Suite

Developed and shipped a suite of **free-to-play poker and casino apps** built in **Unity** for iOS and Android.  
These titles were engineered to support **large-scale audiences** with a strong focus on LiveOps, monetisation, and player retention.

## Overview

The Poker Suite shows how carefully designed **economy systems** and **technical frameworks** can sustain high engagement while meeting business goals.  
Segmented IAP flows, seasonal content pipelines, and cross-promotion features kept players invested, while Unity engineering practices ensured stable and scalable deployment.

## Key Features

- **Segmented IAP Flows** – personalised offers tailored to user segments, increasing ARPU while preserving satisfaction
- **Seasonal Pipelines** – rotating events, challenges, and content drops that kept the experience fresh
- **Cross-Promotion Systems** – interconnected features encouraging players to explore across multiple titles
- **Analytics & A/B Testing** – continuous iteration on monetisation, UX, and engagement loops

## Technical Implementation

- Built with **Unity (C#)** for iOS and Android
- **Firebase Remote Config** for live balancing and feature toggles
- **Unity Addressables** for efficient asset streaming and seasonal swaps
- **Custom build tooling** to reduce release overhead and enable rapid updates
- **Firebase Analytics** for player segmentation and behaviour insights
- **Unity Cloud Build** to support CI/CD and manage multiple titles at once

## Impact

- Sustained **multi-million annual revenue** across the portfolio
- Achieved **99.8% crash-free sessions**, demonstrating technical stability at scale
- Reduced update cycle by **15%** with automated pipelines
- Increased ARPU by **20%** via targeted IAP segmentation
- Cross-promotion drove **higher retention** and install rates across titles

## Future Potential

- Extend framework to additional card and casino genres
- Introduce social features like tournaments, clans, and multiplayer tables
- Apply machine learning for deeper, predictive segmentation
- Further automate LiveOps and seasonal workflows
