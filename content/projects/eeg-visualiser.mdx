# EEG Visualiser (Brainrave)

As **Co-Founder of Brainrave**, I helped create a system that fuses **neuroscience** and **immersive entertainment**.  
The platform captures live EEG data and translates it into dynamic visuals inside Unity, combining shader-driven effects, particle systems, and 3D brain models with reactive audio visualisation.

## Overview

Brainrave transforms a DJ’s brain activity into a live visual performance. EEG signals are captured via BrainFlow, while a custom Rust-based beat detector extracts rhythm data from the music. Both streams are piped into Unity, where adaptive VFX respond in real time to create an immersive, synchronised show.

## How It Works

- **EEG Integration** – Brain signals streamed with **BrainFlow**, processed in Python (smoothing, band-pass filtering, normalisation).
- **Rust Beat Detection** – Real-time audio analysis detects beats/onsets and broadcasts via UDP.
- **Unity Visual Layer** – EEG and beat signals mapped to particle systems, shader morphing, colour changes, and camera motion.
- **Adaptive VFX System** – Modular adapter framework supports rapid development of new visual behaviours, from subtle waveforms to full 3D transformations.

## Why It Matters

Brainrave isn’t just about visuals – it’s about **connecting brain state, emotion, and music** into a single, audience-facing performance. By linking a DJ’s neural data with their live set, the project opens a new frontier for immersive entertainment and **revives live engagement in a post-COVID landscape**.

## Key Features

- Real-time EEG-driven parameter mapping
- Rust-based tempo & beat tracking with millisecond accuracy
- UDP data bridge between neuroscience and Unity runtime
- Shader, particle, and mesh deformation driven by EEG + audio
- Expandable adapter system for new creative VFX behaviours
- Prototypes designed for **VR headsets** and **large-scale LED walls**

## Future Potential

- **AI-based EEG forecasting** to predict brain states in advance
- **Smart-home neurointegration** (mood-driven lighting, environments)
- **Holographic display systems** for large-scale installations
