# Insurance Broker Systems

I contributed to a **full insurance broker system** built in **.NET**, used to run **quotes**, **renewals**, and **claims** for brokers working with **multiple major insurers**. It is a **large, long-lived platform** that covers the bulk of day-to-day broker operations, not a thin quoting widget.

## Overview

The system sits at the centre of broker workflows: from first quote through renewal cycles and into **claims handling**, with data and rules shaped by each **insurer scheme**. Insurers and products in scope included **LV**, **GirlsDriveBetter**, **OneCall**, **Yoga**, **Policywise**, and **Greenlight**, among others — each with its own scheme configuration, validations, and business rules that had to stay accurate as products and regulations evolved.

## Tech Stack

- **Application:** **.NET**, **C#**, **ASP.NET** (typical layered services and data access)
- **Data:** **SQL Server** and scheme-driven configuration for insurer-specific behaviour
- **Operations:** Production support, defect resolution, and incremental delivery alongside BAU

## Role

I **integrated new features** into the live platform, **kept insurance schemes up to date** with changing client and compliance needs, and carried out **support and maintenance** so brokers could rely on the system for high-volume, regulated work.

## Why It Matters

Broker platforms are **mission-critical**: mistakes in pricing, eligibility, or claims data have real customer impact. This work is evidence of **comfort with large .NET codebases**, **domain-heavy logic**, and **sustainable delivery** — shipping improvements without destabilising a system many users depend on.
